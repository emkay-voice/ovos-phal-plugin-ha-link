Link this device to Home Assistant. The device connects to Home Assistant itself, so no port has to be opened, and it works behind a router. In Home Assistant it gets its volume, listening, what was said and answered, and its settings.

Settings

  1. In Home Assistant, install the OpenVoiceOS integration and add it, choosing "An OpenVoiceOS device".
  2. Make a long-lived access token: your profile, Security, at the bottom.
  3. Fill in the address and the token here and save. Then add the device in Home Assistant under Settings, Devices & services, Discovered.
